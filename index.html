privacy

Tessera: Metro Tile Launcher · last updated 15 November 2026

Tessera collects nothing and sends nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it can't send anything anywhere. It has no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting and no accounts. Everything it uses stays on your phone.

what the app reads, and why

Each of these is optional and off until you allow it. The app reads them only to show them on your own screen.

  • Your installed apps: to list them and put them on tiles. The app sees only apps that have a launcher icon.
  • Calendar (if you allow it): your next events on the calendar tile and the me page.
  • Photos (if you allow it): recent photos on the photos tile. A wallpaper you pick is copied into the app's own storage.
  • Contacts (if you allow it): a person's photo on a people tile you pinned.
  • Notification access (if you turn it on): notification counts on tiles, the newest notification on a tile's back, and what's playing on the music tile. Notifications are read on the phone and never stored outside it.
  • Accessibility service (if you turn it on): only to lock the screen when you double-tap start, and to open the notification panel or quick settings when you pull down on start. It receives no events, can't read what's on the screen, and doesn't see what you type.
  • Alarms & reminders and notifications: so a timer or focus session rings on time and shows "time's up".
  • Uninstall requests: "uninstall" in the apps list opens Android's own prompt, which asks you to confirm.
  • Do not disturb, wi-fi state, wallpaper: for the matching tiles and settings.

files you save

When you export a layout or make a backup, it goes where you choose in Android's save dialog (your phone, or a cloud service such as Google Drive if you pick it). Tessera doesn't upload it anywhere itself. That service's own privacy policy applies to files you save there.

android backup

If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android may include Tessera's settings and layout in your phone's backup, like other apps' data. Google handles that backup, not Tessera, and you can turn it off in Android's settings.

children

Tessera is a general home-screen app and collects no data from anyone, including children.
